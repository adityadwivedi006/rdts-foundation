About RDTS Foundation

Working towards a healthier society.

RDTS Foundation is a community-focused organization working towards healthcare awareness, social welfare and initiatives that support the well-being of people.

Our approach focuses on compassion, awareness and meaningful community engagement. We aim to contribute towards better health and improved quality of life.

Healthcare & Community Initiatives

Our work is focused on creating awareness and supporting initiatives that contribute to healthier communities.
